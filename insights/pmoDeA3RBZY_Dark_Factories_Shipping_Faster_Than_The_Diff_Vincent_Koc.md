# Dark Factories: Shipping Faster Than You Can Read The Diff

**Speaker:** Vincent Koc (Maintainer, OpenClaw)

## The Future of Production
- **Dark Factories:** Modern software development is evolving into "dark factories" where autonomous agents ship code faster than humans can read the diffs.
- **Industrial Revolution 2.0:** Transition from being a "weaver" (hand-coder) to a "factory manager" who orchestrates swarms of agents across multiple repositories.

## Velocity at Scale
- **Extreme Velocity:** High-performing teams are reaching astronomical velocities, such as 3,000 commits per day, by leveraging massive agent parallelization.
- **Agent Swarms:** Scale productivity by running dozens of agents in parallel, using sub-agents to handle specialized tasks within a broader "swim lane."

## Quality & Management
- **Taste as Bottleneck:** As coding speed becomes infinite via agents, the primary bottleneck for software quality shifts from labor to human "taste."
- **Vibe Maintenance:** Adopt the role of a "vibe maintainer," focusing on high-level architectural direction and consistency rather than manual line-by-line coding.

## Engineering Logic
- **Smart Looping:** Move beyond simple token burning (Ralph looping) to opinionated reward mechanisms that guide agents toward higher-quality, deterministic outcomes.
- **Invisible Engineering:** Extreme shipping velocity isn't luck; it's the result of rigorous engineering of the agentic harness and reward structures.
