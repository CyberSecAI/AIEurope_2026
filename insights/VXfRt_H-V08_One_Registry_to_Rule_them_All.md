# Overview
## Summary
Amplifon addresses AI chaos by launching the Amplify program, centralizing governance, platforms, and registries for enterprise-scale AI agent and MCP deployments.
## Key Takeaway
Centralized governance and registries are vital for scaling stable, secure, and compliant enterprise AI agent systems.

# Insights
## Scaling Challenges
- Decentralized teams building independent AI connections often lead to fragmented security models and redundant infrastructure chaos across global organizations.
- Organizations must overcome maintenance, compliance, and enterprise scaling hurdles to ensure long-term stability for pervasive AI agent solutions.

## The Amplify Program
- Implement a global, cross-functional operating model to set standard rules for security, legal, and technological AI adoption centrally.
- Use a dedicated control tower and committee to prioritize high-value use cases and ensure strategic alignment across countries.

## Centralized Platform
- Certify unified infrastructures and standardized ways of working to prevent developers from reinventing the wheel for every project.
- Focus platform efforts on providing shared services that simplify the deployment and maintenance of diverse AI use cases.

## AI Gateway
- Provide unified access to model catalogs through a single endpoint to streamline developer interactions with approved AI assets.
- Integrate Entra ID authentication and centralized monitoring to maintain strict security and auditing over all AI agent communications.

## Budget & Resource Control
- Implement granular budget controls at the gateway level to monitor and limit monthly AI spending across various teams.
- Eroding budget displays help developers manage resources responsibly while maintaining visibility into the costs of their AI implementations.

## MCP & Agent Registries
- Build specialized registries for MCP servers and agents to catalog and manage all available tools and their metadata.
- Registries enable agents to discover and interact with each other while maintaining a clear lineage of information and assets.

## Governance & Compliance
- Ensure alignment with AI regulatory requirements by maintaining a centralized catalog of all AI assets and their usage.
- Centralized governance allows organizations to act promptly during disruptions and maintain a consistent lineage for all information flows.
