# AI Product Strategy and Taste

## Summary
As AI commoditizes implementation speed, product taste and knowing what NOT to build becomes the primary competitive differentiator.

## Key Takeaway
Taste is knowing which 90% to throw away; AI amplifies both good and bad judgment equally.

# Insights

## The Productivity Paradox
- AI productivity paradox: engineering friction historically forced validation before implementation, preventing premature feature shipping.
- Token leaderboards measure wrong metric; shipping velocity without strategic filter creates bloated products nobody wants.
- When code generation becomes cheap, choosing what NOT to build becomes the real competitive advantage.

## Quality as Competitive Advantage
- Quality becomes competitive advantage when AI commoditizes feature velocity; taste cannot be automated.
- Design now lags engineering for first time ever; prototypes ship faster than mockups can validate ideas.
- AI amplifies both good and bad judgment equally; teams with poor taste will ship bad products faster than ever before.

## Strategic Decision Making
- Engineering friction historically forced validation before implementation, but AI removes this natural governor.
- The ability to say "no" to features becomes more valuable than the ability to implement them quickly.
- Product strategy requires deeper understanding of user needs when implementation constraints no longer filter ideas.
