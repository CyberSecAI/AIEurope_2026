# Enterprise AI Adoption Patterns

## Summary
Large enterprises build custom internal AI infrastructure including MCP gateways, service discovery integration, and background coding agents while securing competitive positioning.

## Key Takeaway
Big tech's internal AI infrastructure investments reveal competitive dynamics invisible to external observers.

# Insights

## Custom Infrastructure Investment
- Uber, Airbnb, Meta, Microsoft rebuilding complete internal AI infrastructure: monorepo integration, MCP gateways, service discovery.
- Context window limitations for massive codebases force custom solutions over off-the-shelf vendor products.
- Infrastructure investment serves as low-risk AI experimentation ground before shipping customer-facing features.

## Competitive Positioning
- Being six months ahead in AI tooling worth the expense and disruption for tech-focused companies.
- Cannot hire best engineers without offering cutting-edge AI tools; recruitment argument drives adoption.
- Internal tooling investments signal long-term commitment to AI-first development workflows.

## Enterprise Challenges
- Enterprise adoption requires solving identity, security, and governance at scale beyond startup needs.
- Integration with existing enterprise systems (monorepos, service catalogs, security frameworks) creates significant engineering overhead.
- Large organizations need custom MCP gateways and context management solutions that work with their specific architecture.
