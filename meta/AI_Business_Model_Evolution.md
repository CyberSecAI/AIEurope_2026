# AI Business Model Evolution

## Summary
AI reshapes software economics from per-seat SaaS to hybrid pricing, outcome-based value capture, and agent-first monetization patterns.

## Key Takeaway
Hybrid pricing combining base fees with usage-based scaling protects margins while enabling experimentation without usage anxiety.

# Insights

## The Pricing Transformation
- Hybrid pricing adoption exploded from 6% in 2024 to 41% currently, driven by margin protection needs.
- SaaS pricing erodes margins when power users consume 80% compute representing only 5-10% of customers.
- Hypergrowth companies change pricing three-plus times in two years versus low-growth companies at 22%.

## Value-Based Monetization
- Define value from customer perspective using outputs not API calls: automation, augmentation, enhanced service, improved results.
- Outcome-based pricing aligns vendor incentives with customer success rather than resource consumption.
- Agent-first products require rethinking unit economics from human-per-seat to task-per-outcome models.

## Infrastructure and Agility
- Infrastructure choice determines iteration speed; changes requiring months engineering effort kill competitive advantage.
- Fast pricing experimentation requires billing systems designed for flexibility from the start.
- Technical debt in monetization infrastructure prevents companies from adapting to rapidly changing AI economics.
